## F. Art Direction

- **Medium & realism:** Fully computer-generated, photoreal 3D-render look (AI-generated — Inferred). One recurring character: a stylised female skeleton with long wavy auburn hair and an orange/mustard terry headband (sleep mask in S06), often with a translucent glass-like body shell that shows organs. Mostly static or slowly drifting camera; motion comes from character gestures, gas/light effects and one pull-back (S18). Five inside-the-body cutaways (S14, S16, S18, S22, S23) plus the X-ray hook (S01).
- **Palette:** Problem half: warm amber/tungsten domestic interiors, dim bedrooms, with green gas as the recurring symptom signifier and the darkest teal-green in S16. Solution half: introduces bright white tile/white bedding settings (S17, S21, S24, S25, S27) and luminous accents — green-yellow trail (S18), cyan X-ray (S23), gold glow/burst (S25–S26).
- **Typography:** Captions: white bold italic serif with soft drop shadow, centred at ~74% of frame height, 1–4 words per state, sentence punctuation kept, mixed case (one lowercase sentence start, "what"). Headline (S01 only): "Always Feeling" white bold condensed sans + "BLOATED?" gold/yellow bold serif caps, top ~10%. No other graphics, logos, prices or URLs.
- **Transitions:** Hard cuts only (27 shots). Cuts fall on speech pauses in the first half (A21) and on list items (S08–S10, S22–S24). S25→S26 is a hard cut within the same framing, used to start the VFX burst.
- **Product presentation:** Never named in copy. First implied in S17 (dropper at navel, bottle not clearly visible), applied again in S25, and shown clearly only in S27 (amber dropper bottle, cream label, gold collar) for ~1.17 s.
- **Visual signature:** One consistent, slightly comic skeleton heroine with a see-through body acts out each caption line literally in warm, cosy home settings, cut against inside-the-body views of what's going wrong. The problem half is dim, warm and green-gassed; the solution half turns bright white with glowing light effects. White italic serif captions in word-sized bursts carry the narration throughout.
